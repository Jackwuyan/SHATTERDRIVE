# SHATTERDRIVE

A fast-paced neon arcade driving game built around speed,
destruction, drifting, and keeping your combo alive.

## Play

🎮 Play SHATTERDRIVE v0.1 on itch.io:
https://jackwuyan.itch.io/shatterdrive

## About

Smash through targets, drift across the arena, chain hits together,
and push your combo toward x50 SHATTERPOINT.

As your combo climbs, the world reacts — structures awaken,
the arena intensifies, and the run becomes increasingly chaotic.

## Features

- Fast-paced arcade driving
- Destructible targets
- Combo-based world progression
- 180° Drift Burst
- x50 SHATTERPOINT objective
- Adjustable steering feel
- Original neon / synthwave presentation

## Controls

- W — Drive
- A / D — Steer
- S — Brake / Drift
- Hold S and release — Burst
- 180° Drift + release S — Special Drift Burst
